Q1. [MCQ] [1 mark(s)] [EASY] (Category: Layers of Atmosphere)
What is the layer of the atmosphere closest to the Earth's surface where most weather phenomena occur?
Options:
  (A) Stratosphere
      Explanation: The stratosphere is the layer above the troposphere. It contains the ozone layer but is not where most weather occurs.
  (B) Troposphere
      Explanation: Correct. This is the lowest layer of the atmosphere where we live and where weather phenomena like clouds, rain, and wind happen.
  (C) Ozone Layer
      Explanation: The ozone layer is a region within the stratosphere, not a separate primary layer where weather occurs.
  (D) Tropopause
      Explanation: The tropopause is the boundary between the troposphere and the stratosphere, not the layer itself.
Correct Answer: Troposphere
Explanation: The troposphere is the lowest layer of Earth's atmosphere, extending from the surface up to about 6 to 18 kilometers. It is where all land-based plants and animals live and where almost all weather phenomena take place.

Q2. [MCQ] [1 mark(s)] [EASY] (Category: Weather Instruments)
Which instrument is used to measure atmospheric pressure?
Options:
  (A) Thermometer
      Explanation: A thermometer is used to measure temperature.
  (B) Anemometer
      Explanation: An anemometer is used to measure wind speed.
  (C) Barometer
      Explanation: Correct. A barometer measures the weight of the air above us, which is the atmospheric pressure.
  (D) Hygrometer
      Explanation: A hygrometer is used to measure humidity.
Correct Answer: Barometer
Explanation: A barometer is the scientific instrument used to measure atmospheric pressure. Changes in pressure can indicate short-term changes in the weather.

Correct Answer: The five main elements of weather are:
1. Temperature
2. Precipitation

Q6. [MCQ] [1 mark(s)] [MEDIUM] (Category: Wind)
Wind is the movement of air from an area of ___ pressure to an area of ___ pressure.
Options:
  (A) high, low
      Explanation: Correct. Air always moves from a high-pressure system towards a low-pressure system.
  (B) low, high
      Explanation: Incorrect. This is the opposite of how wind is generated.
  (C) high, high
      Explanation: Incorrect. If the pressure were the same, there would be no significant air movement or wind.
  (D) low, low
      Explanation: Incorrect. If the pressure were the same, there would be no significant air movement or wind.
Correct Answer: high, low
Explanation: Air flows from areas where it is more compressed (high pressure) to areas where it is less compressed (low pressure). This movement of air is what we call wind.

The instrument used to measure the speed of the wind is called an ___.
Correct Answer: anemometer
Explanation: An anemometer is the device used for measuring wind speed and is a common weather station instrument. The faster the cups spin, the stronger the wind.

Q8. [SHORT_ANSWER] [2 mark(s)] [MEDIUM] (Category: Atmospheric Pressure)
Why do people sometimes feel breathless at high altitudes like Khardung La?
Correct Answer: At high altitudes, the air gets thinner. This means there is lower atmospheric pressure and less oxygen available in each breath. The body has to work harder to get the oxygen it needs, which can cause feelings of breathlessness, dizziness, or fatigue.
Explanation: Atmospheric pressure is the weight of the air above. At higher altitudes, there is less air above, so the pressure is lower. Lower pressure means the air molecules, including oxygen, are more spread out, making it harder for our lungs to take in the required amount of oxygen.
------------------------------------------------------------

Q9. [MCQ] [1 mark(s)] [MEDIUM] (Category: Weather Instruments)
What is the unit generally used to display atmospheric pressure by a barometer?
Options:
  (A) Celsius (°C)
      Explanation: Celsius is a unit for measuring temperature.
  (B) Kilometres per hour (km/h)
      Explanation: This unit is used for measuring speed, such as wind speed.
  (C) Millimetres (mm)
      Explanation: This unit is used for measuring rainfall depth.
  (D) Millibar (mb)
      Explanation: Correct. This is the standard unit for atmospheric pressure.
Correct Answer: Millibar (mb)
Explanation: The standard unit for measuring atmospheric pressure in meteorology is the millibar (mb). Normal pressure at sea level is about 1013 mb.
------------------------------------------------------------

Q10. [SHORT_ANSWER] [1 mark(s)] [EASY] (Category: Temperature)
What is the 'range of temperature' for a day?
Correct Answer: The range of temperature is the difference between the maximum temperature and the minimum temperature recorded during a particular period, usually 24 hours.
Explanation: To calculate the daily temperature range, you subtract the minimum temperature of the day from the maximum temperature of the day. For example, if the maximum was 32°C and the minimum was 18°C, the range would be 14°C.
------------------------------------------------------------

Q11. [MCQ] [1 mark(s)] [MEDIUM] (Category: Weather Forecasting)
An Automated Weather Station (AWS) is a system that:
Options:
  (A) Requires a meteorologist to be present at all times.
      Explanation: This is incorrect. The purpose of an AWS is to be 'automated' and reduce the need for constant human presence.
  (B) Only measures temperature and rainfall.
      Explanation: This is incorrect. An AWS measures a wide range of data, including temperature, humidity, wind speed and direction, precipitation, and atmospheric pressure.
  (C) Uses various sensors to automatically measure and record weather data.
      Explanation: Correct. This accurately describes the function of an AWS.
  (D) Is only used for predicting earthquakes.
      Explanation: This is incorrect. An AWS is used for monitoring and forecasting weather, not seismic activity.
Correct Answer: Uses various sensors to automatically measure and record weather data.
Explanation: An Automated Weather Station (AWS) is a self-operating system that uses sensors to measure and record weather data like temperature, humidity, wind speed, etc., without the need for human intervention. This provides accurate and timely information.
------------------------------------------------------------

Q12. [SHORT_ANSWER] [2 mark(s)] [EASY] (Category: Weather Forecasting)
Based on the chapter, give two examples of how early humans used to forecast the weather by observing nature.
Correct Answer: Two examples of traditional weather forecasting are:
1. Observing ants shifting their eggs to higher ground, which indicated coming heavy rain.
2. Observing birds flying low, which could signal an approaching storm.
Explanation: Before the invention of modern instruments, people relied on observing patterns in nature. The chapter mentions several examples, including the behavior of ants, birds, squirrels, frogs, and even the opening and closing of pine cones to predict changes in the weather.
------------------------------------------------------------

Q13. [FILL_BLANK] [1 mark(s)] [MEDIUM] (Category: Meteorology)
The systematic study of weather and its evolution, which forms the basis for weather forecasting, is called ___.
Correct Answer: Meteorology
Explanation: Meteorology is the branch of science concerned with the processes and phenomena of the atmosphere, especially as a means of forecasting the weather.
